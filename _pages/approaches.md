# Approaches

The Lab of Cancer Biology uses a translational workflow that begins with patient-derived clinical and omics information and proceeds to molecular validation in experimental cancer models.

## Strategy

1. Identify clinically relevant cancer phenotypes and therapeutic resistance patterns.
2. Analyze patient-derived omics and bio-big data to generate mechanistic hypotheses.
3. Validate candidate drivers and biomarkers using molecular biology assays.
4. Translate mechanistic findings into therapeutic strategies and biomarker concepts.

## Methodological Focus

- Omics data interpretation and bio-big data analysis
- Cancer cell line and molecular biology assays
- Biomarker discovery and validation
- Functional studies of cancer therapy resistance
- Mechanistic studies of immune evasion and metabolic adaptation
