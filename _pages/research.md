    <h1 class="lcb-section-title">Research</h1>
    <p class="lcb-section-lead">
      We are wholly dedicated to acquiring a better understanding of the mechanisms of cancer drug resistance in order to vastly improve patient survival and quality of life.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section class="lcb-section compact">
    <h2 class="lcb-section-title">Research Topics</h2>
    <div class="lcb-card-grid">
      <div class="lcb-card">
        <h3>Identifying Vulnerability of Cancer Cells</h3>
        <p>Discovery of cancer-selective vulnerabilities and therapeutic dependencies for selectively eliminating virulent cancer cells.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="lcb-card">
        <h3>Epigenetic Mechanism for Immune Evasion</h3>
        <p>Understanding how malignant cells epigenetically remodel immune recognition and evade tumor immunity.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="lcb-card">
        <h3>Novel Oncogenic Metabolites</h3>
        <p>Discovering metabolites and metabolic states that support cancer progression and therapy resistance.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="lcb-card">
        <h3>Novel Immunotherapy Modalities</h3>
        <p>Exploring translational strategies including TIL-based therapy and cancer vaccines.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

    </p>

      <div class="lcb-card">
        <h3>Molecular & Cellular Biology</h3>
        <p>Mechanistic validation using cancer models, perturbation experiments, and functional assays.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="lcb-card">
